What is stored, and where

Everything on this page comes out of your browser's own local storage. It never leaves this device: there is no account to create, no cookie that follows you, and no copy of any of it on our side. That also means it does not travel — open DoReDog on your phone and it starts from zero, and clearing your browsing data clears this with it.

Six keys hold the lot: what you pressed play on, your melody-of-the-day history, your best quiz streak, your favourites, your recent pieces and your playlist.
