Why a piano that cannot be wrong

Because pitch and rhythm are two different skills, and beginners are asked to learn both at once. Flow Piano removes one of them completely: the piece's notes are locked in order, so whatever you press, the music comes out right. What is left is the part no notation can write down — when to play, how long to lean on a note, where to breathe. Play the same piece twice and it will sound different both times, because the phrasing is genuinely yours.

Slow it to a crawl and Für Elise turns mournful. Rush it and it panics. That discovery — that you are the tempo — is the fastest way to understand what musicians mean by feeling, and it usually takes years of lessons before anyone lets you have it.

When a piece starts feeling like yours, take the training wheels off: every piece here links back to its real letter-notes player, where the pitches become your job too.
