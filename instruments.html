How can one page play so many instruments?

There are no recordings here and nothing to download. Every sound is built live in your browser with the Web Audio API by stacking harmonics — the recipe of overtones plus the shape of the attack and decay is what makes a marimba sound wooden and a glockenspiel sound like struck metal. A marimba bar, for instance, is tuned so its first strong overtone sits two octaves and a third above the note, which is exactly what its recipe here reproduces.

That means every one of the 2,400+ pieces in the library can be heard on any of these instruments — open a piece and change the Sound control in the player. New to letter notes? Start with the two-minute guide.
