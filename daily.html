Melody of the day

You hear one second. Name the piece in six tries — each wrong guess buys you a little more music. Same melody for everyone, new one at midnight UTC.

#— Heard so far: 1 second

    A new melody every day at midnight UTC. In the meantime: the quizzes never run out.

    Nothing is uploaded and there is no account — your streak lives in this browser. Every melody is played live from the library's letter notes, so whatever comes up today, you can go and learn it.

    How it works

    The melody is chosen by the date, not at random, so everybody in the world gets the same one on the same day — that is what makes a result worth sharing. You start with one second of it. Guess wrong, or press skip, and the clip grows: 1 → 2 → 4 → 7 → 11 → 16 seconds. Six tries in total.

    Guesses come from the real catalogue — start typing a title or a composer and pick from the list. When the puzzle ends you get the answer, a link to play the piece in full, and a little grid you can paste anywhere:

    🟥🟥🟩⬛⬛⬛

    Red is a wrong guess, white a skip, green the one that landed. Your current streak, best streak and solve rate are kept on this device only — clear your browser data and they go with it. That is the whole system; there is nothing to sign up for.

    Where the melodies come from

    The pool is the recognisable end of the library — pieces with an editorial note, cover art, or a place among the featured works. Everything in it is public domain and playable in full: Chopin waltzes, Bach inventions, Mozart sonatas, carols, ragtime, opera arias. If today's melody defeats you, the answer page links straight to its letter notes, which is rather the point.
